Faithfulness is a gate, not a term. Below {{ "%.0f"|format(parameters.gate * 100) }}% a model scores zero however well it covers the document, so a fluent fabrication can never outrank a dull correct summary. Coverage and precision are combined as a harmonic mean (β = {{ parameters.beta }}), which is what stops the score being a length proxy: padding a summary raises coverage but costs precision. A checklist, when supplied, enters as its own term at weight {{ parameters.requirement_weight }}. An em dash means an axis was undefined for that model — not the same as zero.
